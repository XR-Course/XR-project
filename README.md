# About Project

This is a VR fire safety simulation set in an office. First, you inspect the
environment and identify three potential electrical hazards. You can also
continue if you haven't found all of them.

A fire then starts, and you can either evacuate directly or practise using a
fire extinguisher before evacuating. Both paths finish at the assembly area.

To try the simulation, open `Assets/Scenes/OfficeTraining.unity` in Unity and
press Play, or build the enabled scene from Build Profiles.

This is a prototype and should not be presented as certified safety training.

## Controls

- **Grab the extinguisher:** Reach for it or point your controller ray at it,
  then press and hold the grip button on the side of the controller. Release
  the grip button to let go.
- **Use the extinguisher:** While holding it, squeeze and hold the index-finger
  trigger and aim the nozzle at the fire. Keep aiming for about 2.5 seconds
  during the practice phase.
- **Inspect a hazard:** Point the controller ray at it and press the grip button.
- **Press a board button:** Point the controller ray at it and press the
  index-finger trigger.
- **Move:** Use the controller thumbstick to aim a teleport onto the floor.
- **Turn:** Move the controller thumbstick sideways to snap turn.
- **Pause/resume:** Press the controller menu button. On desktop, press Escape.
